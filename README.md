## 🎯 What is YT Cloner?

Moving your YouTube playlists, subscribed channels, or history from one account to another used to be frustrating. Most existing apps charge expensive monthly subscriptions, and browser extensions frequently lock transfers behind paywalls or enforce strict daily limits (such as capping free users at only 50 videos).

**YT Cloner is built differently:** It is **100% free and open-source forever**, with **no ads, no paid subscriptions, and absolutely zero transfer limits**. Clone thousands of videos and channels at maximum speed without spending a penny.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📋 **Bulk Playlist Importer:** Clone and import entire YouTube playlists instantly without tedious one-by-one manual additions.
- 🔔 **Subscription Cloner:** Effortlessly replicate your subscribed channels to another account in just a few taps.
- 🕒 **Watch History Importer:** Restore and backup your YouTube watch history seamlessly via Google Takeout exports.
- ⚡ **Built-in Update Checker:** Tap the app logo at the top anytime to immediately check for and install the latest OTA releases and improvements.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First & Secure:** Runs entirely client-side on your device. Your Google tokens and credentials never leave your phone.
